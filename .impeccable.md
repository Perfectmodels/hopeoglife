## Design Context

### Users
Two distinct audiences:
1. **Public site visitors** — prospective and returning customers of Hope Of Life, a luxury bar lounge & restaurant in Antraco, Angondjé, Libreville, Gabon. Mostly browsing on mobile, in an evening/nightlife decision-making context: deciding whether to book a table, checking the menu/bar card, browsing events, or requesting a privatization.
2. **Restaurant staff** (servers, bartenders, cooks, cashiers, room manager, admin/manager) — using the internal POS dashboard on tablets/desktop terminals during active service, under time pressure. Speed and clarity matter far more than atmosphere here.

### Brand Personality
Luxury bar lounge & restaurant — feutré (hushed, plush), refined, warm gold-on-near-black. Evokes stepping into an elegant space at night: confident, unhurried, a little theatrical without being gaudy.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Public site**: cinematic and unhurried. Slow fades, staggered cascading reveals on scroll, confident/ample motion — never snappy or playful. Existing tokens: near-black background (`#0b0a08`), gold (`#c9a24a`) and champagne (`#e9dcbd`) accents, Playfair Display for headings, Inter for body.
- **Dashboard / POS**: fast, discreet feedback. Short, snappy transitions that acknowledge an action without slowing staff down. Same dark/gold token system, but applied purely for clarity — no "ambiance."
- **Theme**: dark, already established across both surfaces — fits the nightlife/lounge context and night-time service hours.

### Design Principles
1. Site animations should feel like walking into a refined space at night — confident, unhurried motion (300–800ms), staggered reveals, generous easing (ease-out-quart/quint). Never bouncy or playful.
2. Dashboard animations exist purely for feedback and clarity — fast (100–250ms), understated, never delay a staff member's next action.
3. Mobile-first performance on the public site: prefer CSS `transform`/`opacity`, avoid heavy JS animation libraries, respect `prefers-reduced-motion` everywhere.
4. Both surfaces share the same token system (near-black background, gold/champagne accents) but express it differently — atmosphere on the site, efficiency in the dashboard.
